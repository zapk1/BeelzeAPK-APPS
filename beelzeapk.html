iOS is different

Apple doesn't allow an .ipa to be built or signed by a website. iOS apps must be compiled in Xcode on a Mac and signed with a paid Apple Developer account (about $99/year), then installed through TestFlight or the App Store. There is no sideloading an .ipa from a download link on iPhone.
